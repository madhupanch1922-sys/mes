Curriculum

A Balanced, Meaningful Curriculum

Our curriculum combines strong academics with skills and values, so students learn to understand, apply and grow.

01

Strong Foundations

Phonics, reading and number sense are built step by step, so every child masters the basics before moving ahead.

02

Concept-Based Learning

Lessons focus on understanding the “why”, using examples, activities and practice instead of rote memorisation.

03

Hands-On Exploration

Experiments, projects and group tasks make Science, Maths and Social Studies come alive in the classroom.

04

Continuous Assessment

Regular tests, classwork and feedback track each child’s progress and guide timely support.

Classes · Nursery to 10th

A Clear Path, Class by Class

Choose a stage to see how learning grows from play-based beginnings to board-year preparation.

Nursery · LKG · UKG

Pre-Primary

A joyful, play-based start where little learners explore, imagine and build confidence.

  • Play-way learning and sensory activities
  • Phonics, rhymes and storytelling
  • Early numbers, shapes and colours
  • Social skills, manners and motor development
  1. Nursery

    Discovery through play

    Rhymes, colours, sensory play and first words.

  2. LKG

    Letters & numbers

    Phonics, picture reading, counting and pre-writing.

  3. UKG

    Reading readiness

    Simple reading and writing, number operations and curiosity.

Classes 1 – 5

Primary

Core literacy and numeracy are strengthened while curiosity about the world grows.

  • English, Mathematics and Environmental Studies
  • Language learning with reading and writing practice
  • Computer awareness, Art and Physical Education
  • Value education and good habits
  1. Class 1

    Foundation skills

    Reading fluency, handwriting and basic arithmetic.

  2. Class 2

    Growing confidence

    Sentence writing, number operations and EVS.

  3. Class 3

    Exploring concepts

    Comprehension, multiplication and nature studies.

  4. Class 4

    Applying knowledge

    Paragraph writing, problem solving and projects.

  5. Class 5

    Ready for the next stage

    Strong language, maths and Science basics.

Classes 6 – 8

Upper Primary

Subjects deepen and students learn to think critically, question and work independently.

  • English, Mathematics, Science and Social Science
  • Languages with grammar and composition
  • Practicals, charts, models and group projects
  • Computer education and life skills
  1. Class 6

    Building depth

    Subject-wise learning begins with a focus on concepts.

  2. Class 7

    Analysis & reasoning

    Algebra, scientific enquiry, history and geography.

  3. Class 8

    Strengthening the base

    Concept mastery that prepares students for Secondary classes.

Classes 9 – 10

Secondary

Focused academic preparation, revision and guidance for the Class 10 board examination.

  • Core subjects with structured revision
  • Regular unit tests and practice papers
  • Doubt-clearing and extra support sessions
  • Study skills, career awareness and guidance
  1. Class 9

    Exam-oriented foundation

    Detailed syllabus coverage, practicals and regular tests.

  2. Class 10

    Board year

    Revision, mock tests, time management and individual attention.

Co-curricular activities

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Academic excellence and co-curricular activities go hand in hand, shaping confident, creative and caring individuals.

Sports & Games

Athletics, team games and sports day build fitness, discipline and team spirit.

Yoga & Fitness

Morning exercise and yoga improve focus, posture and well-being.

Art & Craft

Drawing, colouring and craft work let creativity take shape.

Music & Dance

Singing, rhythm and dance for cultural programmes and annual events.

Elocution & Debate

Speech, recitation and debate nurture confident public speaking.

Science & Quiz

Exhibitions, models and quizzes spark scientific curiosity.

Green Day & Eco Club

Plantation drives and awareness activities teach care for nature.

Cultural Celebrations

Festivals, Christmas and annual-day programmes celebrate togetherness.
